# The Extraction Ideal — what we actually want (owner-ruled canon, 2026-08-28)

This is the product truth the extraction system serves, independent of any
prompt version. Audits judge against THIS; the prompt is only the current
attempt to induce it. Where prompt and ideal disagree, the prompt is wrong.
Every ruling below is the owner's, made on real corpus cases during the
v17 campaign (loops 1–4); the case that forced each ruling is cited.

## The one job

A user asks "where should I eat / what should I order?" Every stored claim
must be something a real person genuinely vouched for, credited to the
right place, named the way a diner would use it. The two costs are not
symmetric: a WRONG claim (wrong place, dead place, invented dish, junk
entity) poisons trust the first time a user hits it; a MISSED claim is
invisible and recoverable. When forced to choose, miss — but the ideal is
choosing neither.

## What counts as vouching (endorsement)

- Naming a place into a rec thread IS the endorsement — first-hand, by
  restating a name already offered ("Seconding Odd Duck!"), or by bare
  assent behind one ("+1", "Facts", "This is the winner"). Every form is
  the same act and each voucher counts separately: two people vouching is
  twice the evidence, each credited from their own comment. (Adoption
  rulings, loops 2–4.)
- No minimum eloquence: "is good", "solid" standing alone, "their brisket
  slaps" are complete. Mild words read positive standing alone and
  concessive inside a cooler frame ("…but they had a decent taco" after a
  pan withholds). (Position ruling.)
- Habitual choice is endorsement: "I usually get the roast duck", "my
  go-to", "been every year" — repeated return IS the verdict. (Din Ho /
  Fixe rulings.)
- A trip report offering an eaten list as an account (or for judgment —
  "how did we do?") endorses its entries; attendance cited as a
  credential inside an ask does not ("I've only been to Cuba512").
- Fit assertion: an unqualified pick answering a constrained ask asserts
  the ask's ACTUAL venue-level words — cheap, romantic, cuisine included
  (owner ruling: cuisine inherits) — never words the ask didn't say
  (Bartlett's: "not break the bank" licenses affordable, never
  "professional wait staff").
- Value testimony (ruled 2026-08-29): a price or deal OFFERED AS THE
  ANSWER in a value-seeking context is a value claim ("$10 Steak Frites -
  best deal", the kids-eat-free promo reply) and stores as `affordable`;
  a price mentioned in passing asserts nothing. Polarity is absolute:
  "pretty expensive but worth it" never yields affordable. `good value`
  wording folds into the single `affordable` entity.
- Aspect-praise stores as the aspect's strength: "atmosphere is killer" →
  `great atmosphere`, like `good value` (owner ruling). Whole-thing
  praise ("amazing") is the endorsement itself and stores as no attribute.

## What never counts

- Asks (every name inside a request: target, benchmark, comparison
  anchor), plans (including browse-built shortlists — "stood out to me"),
  availability/fact answers (deals, hours, stock — the criterion decides,
  never the verb; quality-criterion asks are judgment asks however
  phrased; non-taste characterization asks — "stoned cooks" — endorse
  nothing), hearsay ("I've heard", giftcard-plus-heard; hearsay CONFIRMED
  by a visit is real), yardsticks (credential lists, comparison losers —
  earn nothing, lose nothing; a writer's own ranked list is all subjects),
  price-only and narration-only clauses, photo reactions, jokes/sarcasm,
  reviews-of-reviews, praise of a person or of the writing.
- Closed places (stated closure — text, remembrance-ask frame, or
  credible hearsay "heard they closed"; a closed BRANCH never closes the
  brand; an object test: "RIP the candy bar" kills no restaurant) and
  dead dishes (verbed removal, in any phrasing; "off the menu" as an
  ordering idiom is alive). Memory never outranks a stated ending.
- Shelf goods: did a KITCHEN make it for you, or did a SHELF hold it?
  Ready-to-eat retail (wine, milk, packaged bars) is still shelf; a good
  finished at home is the writer's kitchen; a product brand is never a
  restaurant. Grocery-mode asks make every pick a shelf answer.
- Out-of-market places (owned by grounding/geography code, not the
  prompt); places named only by URL/@handle (owner ruling: skip).
- Owner self-promotion (ruled 2026-08-28): a business owner or employee
  plugging their own place/product is not word-of-mouth — a
  self-disclosed stake ("my shop", "we just launched") earns nothing,
  however sincere.

## Under measurement (rulings pending data)

- Named-offering dishes (owner-blessed direction 2026-08-29, rederivation
  pending): a NAMED OFFERING YOU COULD ORDER is a dish — omakase, tasting
  menu / chef's tasting, thali, prix fixe, executive lunch, named specials
  and combos. NOT dishes: occasions you attend rather than order (brunch —
  stays an attribute), price-frames ("$25 combo" where the number is the
  point), slots inside an offering ("your choice of meat"), combo-words
  stapling separately-named foods. Open questions the rederivation research
  must settle first: per-restaurant fragmentation ("combo" vs "$25 combo"
  vs named combos at one venue — measure it, confirm the ONE-THING judge
  actually unifies what we assume, decide what merging machinery owns it).

- Cuisine from the venue's own name ("Aha Indian" → indian): the owner
  reopened the ban — intuition says name-derived cuisine is usually
  right. Corpus measurement running (plans/cuisine-name-signal-
  measurement.md); if it holds, the signal joins the KNOWLEDGE side's
  computed restaurant profile deterministically (never per-mention model
  inference).

## Names and dishes

- Names are IDENTITIES: transcribed exactly as written, never corrected,
  completed, or unified in-prompt — unification belongs to the resolver's
  ONE-THING judge (proven live: merges Rudys→Rudy's Bar & Grill, refuses
  Luckys→Lefty's). "&" splits only when both sides carry their own brand.
  Branch phrases ("the south lamar location") resolve to the brand.
- Dishes are also transcribed as-written (no spelling "fixes" — the judge
  unifies "cevichi"/"ceviche"), but a dish is a CONCEPT: singular, menu
  numbering stripped, pro-forms resolved-or-dropped.
- A phrase is a dish when it tells you WHAT ARRIVES (food words: "steak
  combo", "sake pairing"; food-predicting formats: omakase; proper names
  fixing an offering: "the Elvis Presley combo") — emitted AS SPOKEN,
  never peeled to bare terms-words ("pairing") and never truncated past a
  different food ("tri-tip dip" ≠ tri-tip). Terms-only phrases ("lunch
  special", "$25 combo") are venue value-testimony, not dishes. A combo
  word stapling separate things is commentary — each food its own claim.
  A venue's name is never inside a dish name.
- Dishes are born only from vouching clauses; multi-name shared verdicts
  credit EVERY name ("Captain Brad's & Captain Tom's are both great").
- Ingredients: only spans the source wrote (or inside the dish's own
  name); the checker refuses substitutions, not truncations to the noun.

## Cuisine and knowledge

- Extraction NEVER infers cuisine — stated words and fit-asserted ask
  words only, place-side only, filler shed ("great German food" →
  `german`). Derived cuisine belongs to the knowledge system: dish
  concepts get theirs from identity (once, ledgered); restaurants get a
  computed profile (Google + dish set + stated words). Hybrids carry both
  pools (tex-mex → tex-mex AND mexican); bbq is a tradition.

## Systemic ideals (beyond single mentions)

- Provenance is sacred: every claim points at the words that made it
  (place span + source pointer; refusals banked and visible, never
  silent). A pointer is derivable data — repair with a witness, refuse
  only invention.
- Per-mention model judgment is a last resort: anything derivable
  deterministically (canonicalization, geography, unification, cuisine
  derivation, status sweeps) belongs to code or dedicated judges with the
  full picture, bought once and ledgered.
- Chunk-context robustness is a first-class quality axis: behavior must
  not degrade with bundle position (measured lever: docs-per-chunk).
- The audit lens: a finding is not only "violates the prompt" — it is
  also IDEAL-GAP (prompt-obedient but wrong for the product) or
  OPPORTUNITY (signal nobody handles). Those two categories are the
  reason this document exists.
